Manifestation

If you’re aspiring to manifest your dream life you should first understand some basic laws of the universe and psychology . Whether you want to keep it rational and say you act based on the situations you expect or you want to add a bit of spark and say your mind is powerfull enough to create your whole reality and everything is just a reflection of your mind , the outcome is the same : YOU create your life through your thoughts . The way you assume things will happen is the exact way they will happen and if you detach from the feeling of wanting something , it will become a lot easier to attract it in your life .

The real key to manifestation is assertiveness , being clear with what you want and believing there’s no other way of things happening . Below you’ll find all the basic ways to manifest as a beginner , description and guidance through them . You should always keep in mind that there’s no right way to manifest , everyone has their own way but here’s where you can start to find the one that works best for you .

The scripting method is a really powerful way to manifest your desired reality .

So to begin with this technique , you need to get extremely specific with what you want and clarify it in your mind . Then get a pen and paper and write down in detail your desired reality as if it’s happening now or has already happened.Never use words like I want or I will,as the verbs you use set exactly your reality . For example if you write 'I want a car' you're manifesting yourself wanting a car and not having one , so use affirmative words only. Include sight, sound, smell, touch, and taste to make the scene vivid. Write about how you feel in this reality.Also, include words of gratitude.

After finishing fold it towards you to stimulate attraction and read your script daily, ideally in the morning or before bed.After scripting, let go of the “how” and trust the process.Stay open to opportunities and take inspired action.

To manifest using visualisation means to create a clear mental image of your desired reality and combine it with emotion, intention, and inspired action. Your subconscious mind responds strongly to repeated images, especially when they are paired with feeling. Many people combine this with visual boards and meditation where you "daydream" of your desired reality without that feeling of despair.This is not an 100% beginner friendly technique, as you need a lot of focus to do it which is gained through meditation and journalling.

In numerology, each number has a unique vibration and meaning that can help you understand your path and attract what you need. You can find each meaning in the numerology unit . To use them to your benefit you can create a specific affirmation of what you want and write it down or say it out loud a specific amount of times . For instance , many people who want to attract wealth write their manifestations 8 times to attract the abundance number 8 is carrying.

Here are some things you need to always do to prepare yourself for the manifestation . Although , it's common and you're manifesting through your thoughts all the time , you have to always remember that manifestation is like a ritual . You need a crystal clear mind for it to work properly . So by no means should you drink alcohol , endlessly scroll on social media or have negative affairs with anybody before manifesting , because really bad energy can be brought to your thoughts and as a result to your waking life. So always spend some quiet time before , meditate and think clearly of what you want and set your intentions .
