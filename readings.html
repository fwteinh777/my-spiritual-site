A guide to reading the playing cards.

Reading the cards is something that needs a lot of training to learn to do it intuitively and not on autopilot , but first you need to learn and understand what each of the cards mean and where it comes from. Every card is combination of one of the four elements of nature and one numerological meaning.

So let's begin with the elements. Clubs ♣ stand for the air,diamonds ♦ for the earth , hearts ♥ for water and spades ♠ for fire. Below there you can see and tap the card you want to learn about . The key is to start practising messily without extreme structure and to not stop yourself from seeing what the cards have to say just because it's not rational with the knowledge you're holding until this moment.
