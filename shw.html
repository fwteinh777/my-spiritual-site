Shadow Work

Shadow work is maybe the most important aspect of spirituality . It makes you understand yourself so deeply to the point where nothing can disturb you . Let’s get into it .

So to begin with , you have to understand what the shadow self is . It’s the part of our selves that we tend to hide . It was first suggested by the great therapist Carl Jung . “It’s an unconscious part of the human personality containing traits, desires, and emotions that a person rejects or suppresses.” You have to deeply go through your trauma and see your true feelings that might have been transformed . For example , you might have grown resentment for a parent which was not there for you , but the true feelings behind that were abondonment , need for love and depression maybe .

You have to explore this through reflecting and spending time with yourself , asking yourself questions and generally see , understand and love this traits of yours . Also you’re gonna be surprised to understand that not every one of these is bad . It’s just things you haven’t still seen on yourself and trigger you when you see on others . You’ ll find yourself not getting so angry anymore because you will understand how many things were bothering you just because they were you .

Through shadow work you are going to experience ego death . Feelings that are fragile and just exist to protect you are going to disappear and you are gonna feel more in touch with yourself than ever. But ego deaths are very dark. You might find yourself not wanting to be who you once were or not fitting with the people you used to and that’s a huge , depressing maybe , change in your life . The important thing is to not gain spiritual ego and think you’re better than everyone else because you’re doing this .

So to start off you need to open your pinterest or find an online book ( I’ll have some suggestions below ) and search shadow work prompts . It’s not gonna be easy , but the self love you will gain is gonna be worth every moment . You have to be completely honest with yourself, make sure you are copletely alone and always remember to give time to yourself to process what you have just realised , grieve and release the old part of you . Shadow work is not a linear routine , it’s a self discovery journey that’s gonna shock you more often than not . So good luck , stay strong and remember you’re not doing this to find more things to hate yourself for , you’re doing it to accept and love yourself deeply.
